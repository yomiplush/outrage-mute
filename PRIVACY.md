# Privacy Policy — outrage-mute (義憤ミュート)

**Effective date:** 2026-10-15

## What this extension does

outrage-mute analyzes posts on X (x.com / twitter.com) locally on your device
and blurs or hides posts that score as "outrage" (aggressive, inflammatory, or
hostile wording). It uses a local dictionary and heuristics — no machine
learning, no API keys, and no account required.

## Data we collect

**None.** This extension does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any
personal data.

All processing happens entirely on your device. The extension **makes no
network requests** and sends no data — including posts, browsing history, or
settings — to any server.

## Permissions used

- **`storage`** — used only to save your settings and daily statistics in
  Chrome's local storage on your device.
- **Host access to `x.com` and `twitter.com`** — required only to detect and
  hide posts on those sites. No data from these pages is transmitted anywhere.

## Data stored locally

Your settings (thresholds, category toggles, mute words, and statistics) are
stored **only on your device** in Chrome's local storage
(`chrome.storage.local`). We cannot access this data, and it is removed when
you uninstall the extension.

## Third-party services

This extension uses no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
